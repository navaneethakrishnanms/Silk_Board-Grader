All values live in `backend/engine/silkgrade_engine/config.py`.

## Colour measurement and resolution (WP4.7)

The engine analyses images at most `ANALYSIS_MAX_SIDE = 1600` px (area-averaged), **including the colour
measurement**. The brief says to downscale only for registration and clustering if grading is too slow.
Measured on the stand-in sheet: the median colour of every piece differs between full resolution and the
analysis scale by at most **0.0006 ΔE00** at 1600 px (0.0013 at 800 px) — over 1000× smaller than the
smallest grey-scale step (0.8). Reason: the pipeline already averages texture with a 1 mm Gaussian, and
area-averaging to 1600 px is a finer average than that. So the measurement is unchanged and grading stays
fast: **3.7 s** for specimen + 2 adjacent pieces on the full 2400×1700 stand-in sheet.
**Re-check on the real scans**: a 600-dpi A4 scan is ≈ 35 MP; if `tools/tune_client_scans.py --time-grading`
reports > 5 s, lower `ANALYSIS_MAX_SIDE` only after re-running the same full-res vs analysis comparison.

## Known limits (stand-ins)

* `blur` fires on the stand-in light-blue strip and satin: their synthetic weave is smoother than real
  silk. `BLUR_MIN = 60` was **not** changed without real scans.
* Thin print elements (stems) keep few measuring pixels after `CLUSTER_BOUNDARY_ERODE_PX = 2`; on real
  600-dpi scans they are wider in pixels. Re-check the green-stem cluster on the real floral piece.
