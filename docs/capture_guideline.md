# SilkGrade — Image Capture Guideline (for the laboratory)

SilkGrade measures colour from your photos. The app can only be as accurate as the images,
so please follow these rules for **every** test. The same text is shown in the app
(Upload screens → "Capture guideline") and can be edited by an admin in Settings.

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|------|-----|
| C1 | Take the before-wash and after-wash images with the **same device**. | Different cameras render colour differently. |
| C2 | Use the **same lighting** for before and after — ideally a D65 viewing booth. | A lighting change looks like a colour change. |
| C3 | Turn **auto-enhancement, HDR, beauty mode and filters OFF**. On scanners, turn auto-levels / colour restoration OFF. | Processing alters colour and clips whites. |
| C4 | Make sure the white adjacent fabric is **not over-exposed** (no pure-white areas). | Faint staining is lost when whites clip. |
| C5 | Lay the fabric **flat** — no folds, wrinkles or shadows. | Folds change the measured colour. |
| C6 | Use a **neutral grey or black background**. Never white. | A white background is confused with white fabric. |
| C7 | Recommended: include a small **colour reference card** (24-patch colour checker) in every image. | Lets the app correct small lighting and device differences. |
| C8 | Recommended: photograph an **unwashed piece of each adjacent fabric** together with the after-wash set. | It is the staining baseline. |
| C9 | At least **3 MP of fabric area**. Save as JPEG (maximum quality), PNG, TIFF or DNG. | Enough pixels to average the weave texture. |
| C10 | Either **one image per piece**, or **all pieces in one image** with clear gaps between them. | Both modes are supported. |

## Practical checklist

1. Fix the phone/camera on a stand at the same height every time; lock focus and exposure if possible.
2. Phone settings: disable HDR, "scene optimiser", AI enhancement and beauty filters; use the highest JPEG quality.
3. Place the composite pieces flat on the grey/black board with ~1 cm gaps.
4. Place the colour reference card at the side of the frame, fully visible and flat.
5. Before uploading, check the app's warning chips: **Blur**, **Clipped whites**, **Glare**, **Low resolution**.
   Retake the photo if any appear.
6. After washing, use the **same setup, device and lighting** — the app warns when the device differs.

Grades are *instrument-assisted* and remain provisional until the laboratory completes method validation.
